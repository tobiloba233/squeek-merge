    About Squeek Merge

    Merge hamsters on a 5×5 board while the real Squeektoshi runs on the WHEELY9000. Squeekchain is a hamster-powered proof-of-work toychain on Ethereum Sepolia.

    How to play

    • Tap a Squeek, then tap a neighbour of the same level to merge them. The survivor moves up one level.
    • A freshly merged Squeek rests for 2 turns (zz) and cannot merge again, like a Squeek on the real chain.
    • One new Pup appears every turn. No merge available? Press Nap to pass the turn.
    • Reach Genesis (level 8) to win. Genesis is the top level and does not merge. Keep playing for score.
    • Bigger merges score more. Merging on back-to-back turns builds a combo.
    • You get 3 undos per run. The run ends when the board is full and nothing can merge.

    Live wheel bonuses

    • Power Surge: the wheel is running. All merge points are doubled.
    • Nap Mode: the wheel is resting. Normal points. Naps are good too.
    • Block Bonus: a new Squeek block lands while you play. Two Pups are cleared from the board.
    • If the wheel can't be reached, the game plays normally without bonuses. The Squeektoshi live panel then offers a simulated demo so you can see each bonus. Demo runs never count toward Best.

    Keys

    Arrow keys move, Enter or Space selects, U undoes, N naps.

    Notes

    Unofficial fan game. Testnet only, no monetary value. It reads public, read-only data from api.squeekchain.com. No wallet, no payments, no tracking. It never asks the real hamster to run.
